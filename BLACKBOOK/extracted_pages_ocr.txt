CHAPTER 1 INTRODUCTION Parking has become one of the most pressing issues in modern cities. With the increase in urbanization, population growth, and the rising number of vehicles on roads, the problem of finding an available parking slot has intensified. In most cases, drivers waste a significant amount of time and fuel searching for free parking slots. This not only leads to personal frustration but also contributes to traffic congestion, increased pollution, and inefficient resource usage. Conventional parking management systems depend heavily on manual monitoring, ticketing, and supervision, which are prone to human errors and inefficiencies. Moreover, many of the existing automated systems are based on licensed software that comes with very high setup and maintenance costs. Smaller parking areas such as those in local markets, offices, and community spaces cannot afford such solutions. To address this problem, we have developed an open-source parking slot detection and online booking platform. The system uses computer vision models integrated with CCTV cameras to monitor parking lots, detect available slots, and recognize vehicle number plates. In parallel, a user-friendly web-based booking platform allows drivers to check availability and reserve their slot before reaching the parking lot. The uniqueness of our solution lies in its cost-effectiveness and open-source adaptability, which makes it accessible for both large and small parking operators. By eliminating the dependency on licensed, expensive software, the system democratizes access to smart parking management. The solution is scalable, reliable, and contributes to reducing human effort, operational costs, and environmental impact.

For society, it reduces unnecessary vehicle movement, thereby lowering traffic congestion and fuel wastage. • For future scalability, the system can be integrated with IoT devices, payment gateways, and smart navigation apps like Google Maps. 2

CHAPTER 2 SURVEY OF TECHNOLOGIES Available Technologies A survey of existing parking solutions revealed that while advanced parking systems are available, they come with significant drawbacks: 1. High Licensing Costs: Most proprietary software solutions require costly licenses, making them unsuitable for small parking operators. 2. Complex Hardware: Advanced sensors and high-end devices increase installation and maintenance costs. 3. Limited Flexibility: Proprietary systems are less customizable and lock customers into vendor-specific hardware/software. In contrast, our project leverages open-source technologies such as YOLO (computer vision), Streamlit (rapid prototyping), and Flask (deployment), combined with affordable hardware like CCTV cameras. This allows us to provide similar functionality at a fraction of the cost, with more flexibility. Technologies Used • YOLO (You Only Look Once): Used for real-time object detection, specifically to recognize empty and occupied parking slots, as well as number plate recognition. OpenCV: For processing video input from CCTV cameras and integrating detection models. • Streamlit: Used to create the prototype user interface for testing booking and slot availability features. • Flask: For building the backend APIs and enabling platform deployment. RDBMS (MySQL/PostgreSQL): To store user records, booking transactions, slot availability, and historical data. These technologies collectively provide a robust and affordable smart parking solution. 4
